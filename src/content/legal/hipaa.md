## Important Notice

{% callout %}
**This Notice describes how medical information about you may be used and disclosed, and how you can get access to this information. Please review it carefully.**
{% endcallout %}

Premier Wellness Telehealth, LLC is required by federal law (HIPAA) to maintain the privacy of your protected health information (“**PHI**”), to provide you with this Notice of our legal duties and privacy practices with respect to PHI, and to follow the terms of the Notice currently in effect.

## Who Follows This Notice

This Notice applies to the privacy practices of Premier Wellness Telehealth, LLC, including:

- The nurse practitioner and any other licensed clinicians who provide care under our practice
- Employees, contractors, and trainees who assist with care or administration
- Business associates who handle PHI on our behalf (such as our electronic medical record provider and laboratory partners)

All of these individuals and entities are required to follow this Notice.

## How We May Use and Disclose Your PHI

We may use and disclose your PHI without your written authorization for the following purposes:

### For Treatment

We use your PHI to provide medical care — for example, to evaluate symptoms, make a diagnosis, prescribe medications, order labs, and document your visit. We may share PHI with other healthcare providers who are involved in your care, such as your primary care provider, specialists, pharmacies, or laboratories.

### For Payment

We may use your PHI to obtain payment for the services we provide. Because we operate on a cash-pay model, this typically means generating receipts and processing your payment. If you choose to submit a receipt to your insurance company for reimbursement, that activity is between you and your insurer.

### For Healthcare Operations

We may use your PHI for activities necessary to run our practice — for example, quality review, training, scheduling, billing operations, and legal and regulatory compliance.

## Other Permitted Uses Without Your Authorization

Federal and state law permit or require us to use or disclose your PHI without your authorization in certain limited situations, including:

- **Public health activities** (such as reporting communicable diseases or adverse drug events)
- **Health oversight** (audits, investigations, and licensure activities)
- **Legal proceedings** (in response to a court order, subpoena, or other lawful process)
- **Law enforcement** (when required by law or in response to a valid request)
- **Suspected abuse, neglect, or domestic violence** (when required to be reported)
- **To avert a serious threat** to your health or safety or that of others
- **Workers’ compensation** (to the extent required by state law)
- **Coroners, medical examiners, and funeral directors**
- **Specialized government functions** (military, national security, protective services)

## Uses That Require Your Written Authorization

Most other uses and disclosures of your PHI will be made only with your written authorization. In particular, we will obtain your authorization before:

- Using or disclosing your PHI for **marketing** purposes
- **Selling** your PHI
- Using or disclosing any **psychotherapy notes**, if applicable

You may revoke a written authorization in writing at any time, except to the extent we have already acted in reliance on it.

## Your Rights Regarding Your PHI

You have the following rights with respect to your PHI:

### Right to inspect and copy

You may inspect and request a copy of your PHI that we maintain. We may charge a reasonable, cost-based fee for copies as permitted by law.

### Right to request an amendment

If you believe information in your record is incorrect or incomplete, you may request that we amend it. We may deny your request in certain circumstances and will explain our reasons in writing.

### Right to an accounting of disclosures

You may request a list of certain disclosures of your PHI that we have made, other than disclosures for treatment, payment, or healthcare operations and certain other exceptions.

### Right to request restrictions

You may request a restriction on how we use or disclose your PHI for treatment, payment, or healthcare operations. We are not required to agree to most restriction requests, but we will accommodate reasonable requests when feasible. If you pay out of pocket in full for a particular item or service, you may request that we not disclose PHI about that item or service to your health plan, and we will honor that request as required by law.

### Right to confidential communications

You may request that we contact you in a specific way (for example, by a particular phone number or email address). We will accommodate reasonable requests.

### Right to a paper copy of this Notice

You may request a paper copy of this Notice at any time, even if you have agreed to receive it electronically.

### Right to be notified of a breach

You have the right to be notified in the event of a breach of unsecured PHI that affects you.

## Our Duties

We are required by law to:

- Maintain the privacy and security of your PHI
- Provide you with this Notice of our legal duties and privacy practices
- Notify you if a breach occurs that may have compromised the privacy or security of your PHI
- Follow the terms of the Notice currently in effect

## Changes to This Notice

We reserve the right to change this Notice and to make the new Notice apply to PHI we already have, as well as any information we receive in the future. When we make material changes, we will post the revised Notice on our website and make copies available at our practice.
