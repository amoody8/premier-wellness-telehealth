question: What conditions do you treat?
order: 5

Premier Wellness Telehealth provides non-emergency virtual care for adults age
{{ site.minimumAge }} and older. Services include preventive wellness care,
medication management, lab review and follow-up, selected stable chronic
conditions, women's health concerns, and common acute illnesses.

Examples include hypertension, type 2 diabetes, high cholesterol, stable asthma or
COPD, upper respiratory infections, sinus symptoms, seasonal allergies,
uncomplicated urinary symptoms, conjunctivitis, headaches, nausea, vomiting,
diarrhea, constipation, selected skin concerns, yeast symptoms, and contraception
management.

Weight-management treatment may also be offered when medically appropriate,
including evidence-based prescription therapy for eligible patients.

Some conditions require laboratory testing, an in-person examination, imaging,
specialist care, or a higher level of care, and therefore may not be appropriate
for virtual management. [See all services]({{ "/services/" | url }}).
