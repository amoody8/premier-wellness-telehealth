---
question: Who shouldn't use telehealth?
order: 6
---

Telehealth is not appropriate for medical emergencies or symptoms that may require
immediate hands-on evaluation. **Call 911 or seek emergency care** for symptoms such
as severe chest pain, severe shortness of breath, signs of stroke, loss of
consciousness, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, or another
potentially life-threatening condition.

We may also refer patients for in-person or specialty care when a condition is too
complex or high-risk to manage safely through virtual care. This may include
pregnancy-related care, advanced kidney disease, heart failure, atrial fibrillation
or other complex arrhythmias, active cancer treatment, complex autoimmune disease,
or other conditions requiring ongoing specialty management.

Controlled substances are not prescribed through the practice.

If a virtual evaluation shows that an in-person examination, testing, imaging,
emergency care, or specialist evaluation is needed, you will be advised of the
appropriate next step.
