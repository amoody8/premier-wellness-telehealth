Common Questions

Good to know.

Have a question we haven't answered? Reach out — we love helping new patients understand what to expect.

What states do you serve?+
We currently provide telehealth services to patients located in Florida, Arizona, and Washington. State telehealth regulations require that you be physically present in one of these states at the time of your visit.
Do you take insurance?+
At this time, Premier Wellness Telehealth operates on a cash-pay model. This keeps pricing transparent and avoids the delays and limitations of insurance billing. Many patients use HSA or FSA cards for visits, and we provide receipts you can submit to your insurance for possible reimbursement.
What kinds of conditions can be treated by telehealth?+
We treat low-acuity acute illnesses (cold, flu, UTI, allergies, sinus infections) as well as chronic conditions like hypertension, diabetes, hyperlipidemia, and thyroid disorders. We also offer medication management, lab review, and preventive wellness visits. Conditions requiring in-person evaluation will be referred appropriately.
Can you prescribe medication?+
Yes. We prescribe non-controlled medications including antibiotics, blood pressure medications, thyroid medications, diabetes medications, and more. Prescriptions are sent electronically to the pharmacy of your choice. We do not prescribe controlled substances at this time.
How do I get labs done?+
If labs are needed, your provider will order them electronically through Labcorp or Quest Diagnostics. You complete the labs at a convenient local location, and we review results with you at a follow-up visit.
What if I need to be seen in person?+
If your situation requires hands-on care, we'll help you find an appropriate in-person resource — urgent care, primary care, or the emergency department, depending on what's needed. Your safety always comes first.
