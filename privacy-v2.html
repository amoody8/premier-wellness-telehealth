Section 01Overview

Premier Wellness Telehealth, LLC (“Premier Wellness,” “we,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes what information we collect through this website (premierwellnesstelehealth.com), how we use it, and the choices you have.

This policy applies only to information collected through our public website. It does not cover information you provide once you become a patient and use our scheduling and clinical platform — that information is handled under our Notice of Privacy Practices (HIPAA) and the privacy practices of our electronic medical record provider.

Section 02Information We Collect

Through this website, we collect very little information about you. Specifically:

Information you give us

If you email us or use a contact form, we receive whatever information you choose to share — typically your name, email address, and the content of your message. Please do not include any details about your health, symptoms, conditions, or medical history when contacting us through the website or by general email. Those conversations belong inside our secure clinical platform.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider and analytics tools may automatically collect basic technical information when you visit, such as:

  • Your IP address and approximate geographic region
  • Browser and device type
  • Pages you visit and the time and date of your visit
  • The website you came from, if any

This information is used to keep the site secure, troubleshoot issues, and understand how visitors use the site in aggregate.

Section 03How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide requested information
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the website
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical or security issues
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or marketing to third parties.

Section 04How We Share Information

We share limited information only with service providers that help us run this website — for example, our hosting provider, our email provider, and (if used) an analytics provider. These providers may only use the information to provide their services to us.

We may also disclose information if required by law, in response to a valid legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Premier Wellness, our patients, or others.

Section 05Booking and the EMR

When you click a “Book Appointment” button or otherwise schedule a visit, you are redirected to our electronic medical record (EMR) provider’s booking page. The EMR provider operates that page; information you submit there is governed by the EMR’s privacy practices and our Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with them, not by this Privacy Policy.

Plain summary:The marketing website you are reading does not collect any health information. Once you click “Book,” you leave this site, and protected health information you provide is governed by HIPAA.

Section 06Protected Health Information

Protected Health Information (PHI) is handled separately from website data under federal HIPAA rules. For details on how we use and protect your PHI, please read our Notice of Privacy Practices.

Section 07Cookies and Analytics

This website may use a small number of cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences and understand how the site is used. If we use a web-analytics service, we configure it not to track personally identifiable information.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. Doing so will not prevent you from using the site, but some features may not work as expected.

We do not currently use advertising or social-media tracking pixels on this website.

Section 08Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights with respect to your personal information — including the right to know what we collect, request a copy, request correction, or request deletion. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information at the bottom of this page.

Residents of certain states (including Florida, California, and Washington) may have additional rights under state privacy laws. We honor verifiable consumer requests from residents of those states to the extent required by law.

Section 09Children’s Privacy

This website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Section 10Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information collected through this website, including encryption in transit (HTTPS) and access controls on our administrative tools. No website, however, can be guaranteed to be 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of information transmitted to or from the site.

Section 11Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Material changes will be noted prominently on the site. Please review this policy periodically.
